# Adopters

This file lists organisations and projects that use LLM4S. Knowing who uses it helps the maintainers decide what to
prioritise, and helps newcomers judge whether the library fits their situation.

Listing is **voluntary and by pull request**. The maintainers do not add anyone on their own, and an entry says only
what its authors wrote about themselves. LLM4S makes no claim about any entry and does not endorse any of them.

## How to add yourself

Open a pull request that adds a row to the table below, keeping the rows in alphabetical order by name, with:

- **Name**: your organisation or project.
- **Link**: a public page (a website or repository) where a reader can learn about you.
- **Use case**: one short line on what you use LLM4S for (for example "internal support assistant" or "RAG over our
  documentation"). Leave out anything confidential.
- **Since**: the month and year you started using it (optional).
- **Modules**: the `llm4s-*` modules you depend on (optional).

You can also describe your use on [Discord](https://discord.gg/4uvTPn6qww) first, and a maintainer can help turn it
into an entry.

Sign your commits as for any contribution (`git commit -s`, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

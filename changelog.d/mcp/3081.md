- **Fixed** `MCPServer` conformance: `ping` is answered, sessions expire after `sessionIdleTimeout` (30 min) with at most `maxSessions` (1000), versions 2025-06-18, 2025-03-26 and 2024-11-05 are accepted (a request without the header is 2025-03-26),
  tool annotations are advertised (new `annotations` constructor parameter), and `notifications/cancelled` interrupts a running tool call, whose reply is then not sent ([#3081](https://github.com/llm4s/llm4s/issues/3081)).

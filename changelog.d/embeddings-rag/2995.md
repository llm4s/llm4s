- **Fixed** `SQLiteKeywordIndex` no longer fails on quotes, operator words or a blank query, ORs a query's words so a question finds a chunk lacking some of them, and filters as `SQLiteVectorStore` does
  ([#2995](https://github.com/llm4s/llm4s/issues/2995); [Guide](docs/guide/vector-store.md#fusion-strategies)).
- **Added** `LegFailurePolicy`: an RRF or weighted search whose vector or keyword leg fails answers from the other with a WARN (`Degrade`, the default) or fails (`Fail`), on `HybridSearcher`, `AsyncHybridSearcher` and `RAGConfig`
  ([#2995](https://github.com/llm4s/llm4s/issues/2995)).

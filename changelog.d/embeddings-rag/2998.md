- **Fixed** `DirectoryLoader` reports a root or subdirectory it cannot list, or an entry whose real path it cannot read, as a `ListingFailure`, so `RAG.sync`
  deletes nothing under it rather than every document there ([#2998](https://github.com/llm4s/llm4s/issues/2998); [Guide](docs/guide/document-loaders.md#directoryloader)).

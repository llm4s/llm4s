- **Fixed** giving a leaf collection a child no longer hides its documents from every search: a leaf holding documents is refused a child, the demotion and the insert are one transaction,
  and `PgSearchIndex` searches non-leaf collections, so documents an earlier version hid are found again ([#2991](https://github.com/llm4s/llm4s/issues/2991); [Guide](docs/guide/permission-based-rag.md#hierarchical-structure)).

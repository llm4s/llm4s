- **Fixed** the Postgres stores' metadata filters (`PgVectorStore`, `PgKeywordIndex`, `PgSearchIndex`) select what `SQLiteVectorStore` does: `HasKey` runs, `Not` keeps a record without the key,
  `Contains` is a literal substring test, and an empty `In` or `Not(All)` matches nothing; `SQLiteKeywordIndex`'s `Contains` and `Not` likewise ([#2996](https://github.com/llm4s/llm4s/issues/2996); [Vector stores](docs/guide/vector-store.md#combining-filters)).

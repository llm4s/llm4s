- **Changed** the `openai`, `azure`, `requesty`, `anthropic` and `bedrock` clients send one request per call: their vendor SDK's own retries (3 attempts, 4 on Bedrock) are off, so under
  `ReliableClient` a call sends at most its `RetryPolicy`'s attempts and `timeouts.request` bounds each ([#3210](https://github.com/llm4s/llm4s/issues/3210); [Guide](docs/guide/reliability.md)).

## LLM4S Dev Hour

<img class="events-banner" src="{{ '/image_assets/llm4s_dev_hour/banner/llm4s-dev-hour-banner.png' | relative_url }}" alt="LLM4S Dev Hour banner">

A weekly **mob programming** session where we code, debug and learn together, open to everyone, whether it is your
first contribution or your hundredth.

- **When:** every Sunday at 09:00 London time. Next session: <span data-dev-hour-next>Sunday, 09:00 London time</span>.
  The [Dev Hour time zone table](/community/#llm4s-dev-hours) gives the time in other cities.
- **Where:** the LLM4S [Discord](https://discord.gg/4uvTPn6qww). Each session is listed on the
  [Luma calendar](https://luma.com/calendar/cal-Zd9BLb5jbZewxLA), so you can add it to yours.
- **Hosts:** Kannupriya Kalra and Rory Graves. The host posts updates in the `#llm4s-dev-hour` Discord channel after
  each session.

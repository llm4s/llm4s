# GSoC 2026
{: .fs-9 }

Join LLM4S for Google Summer of Code 2026 and build the future of AI in Scala.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

LLM4S is proud to participate in Google Summer of Code 2026 through the Scala Center. We are offering 75+ project ideas across multiple domains including AI Agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Data Pipelines, Hardware Design, and more.

---

## Quick Start Guide for Aspirants

### Step-by-Step Application Process

1. **Join the Community**
   - [Join our Discord](https://lnkd.in/eb4ZFdtG)
   - Introduce yourself in `#introduce-yourself` channel
   - Add yourself to the [GSoC 2026 Aspirants spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1GzQDQaFCp4iJz8WIMwiooVDk0JWPh6hvODWRzEZRHxY/edit?gid=0#gid=0)

2. **Understand LLM4S**
   - Read through [llm4s.org](https://llm4s.org/)
   - Explore the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/llm4s/llm4s)
   - Check the [production roadmap](../../reference/roadmap.md) and [hardware design roadmap](../../roadmap/llm4s-hardware-design-roadmap.md)

3. **Get Hands-On**
   - Look for "good first issues" on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/llm4s/llm4s/issues)
   - Set up the dev environment: Install JDK 21+ and SBT
   - Run `sbt test` to verify your setup
   - Create issues proactively based on the roadmap
   - Submit pull requests (notify maintainers in `#pr-review` Discord channel)

4. **Engage with the Community**
   - **Attend LLM4S Dev Hours** every Sunday at 9am London time
     - [LLM4S Dev Hours](https://lnkd.in/e722aSVt) - Weekly changing link
   - Ask questions in Discord channels
   - Engage in pair-programming setups
   - Help other aspirants (shows initiative and leadership)

5. **Proposal Writing**
   - Browse our [project ideas](#project-ideas)
   - Discuss ideas with mentors via email or Discord
   - Study the [GSoC proposal writing guide](https://google.github.io/gsocguides/student/writing-a-proposal)
   - Focus on Type Safety, Functional Programming, and AI innovation
   - Submit your proposal through the official GSoC website

6. **Professional Development**
   - Update your LinkedIn to showcase contributions
   - Tag `@LLM4S Foundation` when posting about your work
   - Blog about your learning journey
   - Contribute to open-source to build your portfolio

## Requirements & Guidelines

### Technical Requirements

- **Language**: Scala (Scala 2.13 and 3.7.1 support)
- **Build Tool**: SBT (Scala Build Tool)
- **JDK Version**: JDK 21+ recommended
- **Testing Framework**: ScalaTest for unit tests
- **Code Quality**: Must pass scalafmt (formatting) and scalafix (linting)

### Application Requirements

From Google: [Writing a Proposal](https://google.github.io/gsocguides/student/writing-a-proposal)

**Key Points for LLM4S Proposals:**
- Be specific about deliverables and success criteria
- Show understanding of the codebase through referenced PRs or issues
- Demonstrate functional programming knowledge
- Explain how your project improves type safety or developer experience
- Include a realistic timeline with milestones

### Important Program Rules

The GSoC program prioritizes:

- ✅ **Learning Experience**: We seek to develop the next generation of developers
- ✅ **Equal Opportunity**: We prioritize disadvantaged backgrounds and early-career developers
- ✅ **Commitment**: This is not a freelance job; it's a mentorship program
- ✅ **Participation**: Regular attendance at Dev Hours and community engagement is expected

→ [Full GSoC Rules & Terms](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/rules)

## How to Get Selected

### Competitive Advantages

1. **Prove your commitment early**
   - Make meaningful contributions before GSoC applications open
   - Reviewers look for evidence of interest and skill
   
2. **Engage with the community**
   - Attend Dev Hours regularly
   - Help other aspirants in Discord
   - Ask thoughtful questions in discussions
   
3. **Show initiative**
   - Create issues based on the roadmap
   - Submit PRs for improvements you identify
   - Discuss your ideas with mentors beforehand
   
4. **Understand the domain**
   - Learn about LLMs and Scala thoroughly
   - Read documentation and blogs
   - Try example projects from the codebase
   
5. **Communication skills**
   - Write clear, professional proposals
   - Describe your background and motivation
   - Be clear about what you'll deliver

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Generic proposals**: Copy-pasting or vague descriptions
- **No prior engagement**: No PRs, issues, or Discord activity
- **Unrealistic timelines**: Promising too much in 175-350 hours
- **Ignoring feedback**: Not incorporating mentor suggestions
- **Poor communication**: Vague or unclear about your plans

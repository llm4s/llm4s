Support the foundation

Keep type-safe AI on the JVM open, maintained and free

LLM4S is community-led and maintained by a small core team, mostly in volunteer time. Your support pays for maintainer time, contributor onboarding, documentation, release engineering, CI, security hardening, the weekly Dev Hour and internships. Every contribution is handled in public through Open Collective.
